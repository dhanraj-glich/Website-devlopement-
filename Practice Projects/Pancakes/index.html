Simple Pancakes

Soft, fluffy, and easy to make pancakes perect for a quick breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg and milk.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  5. Pour some batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook the other side.
  8. Serve hot with honey, syrup, or fruit
