About the coach

I got into this because I hated how "fitness advice" online never fit real schedules.

Ten years ago I was a mechanical engineering student who couldn't do a single pull-up. Today I coach IT professionals, founders and students across Pune — most of whom sit 9+ hours a day and just want to move well, get strong, and stop getting injured.

My approach is simple: fewer, better exercises, tracked properly, adjusted every 2 weeks based on your actual recovery — not a generic PDF plan.
